# Minimal Tier

Bug fix, small follow-up, or work where context is one ticket-link away. Cues: "fix", "follow-up to PROJ-NNN", "tiny change", "implements PROJ-NNN" (spike). Word budget: **~50-150 words** for the description.

## Sections to compose

Just one section in the Description field, read its guidance before drafting:

1. Summary -- `.rulesync/docs/jira/ticket-sections/summary.md`

Plus Acceptance Criteria (dedicated JIRA field, not the description) -- `.rulesync/docs/jira/ticket-sections/acceptance-criteria.md`.

Skip Background, Approach, Out Of Scope, Downstream Impact, and Future Work unless the linked ticket truly doesn't cover them. If it does, the link is doing the work -- repeating it here is noise.
