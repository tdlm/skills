## 1. Slack: why a PR is taking long

I've taken multiple swings at this one, so it's taken a bit longer than other PRs normally would. The first two attempts were just hot garbage, and I didn't feel comfortable releasing that code into the world.

## 2. Opinion / recommend

Buy a Tesla if you value simplicity and don't want to worry about maintenance. Also, if you like being able to control your car remotely! For example, I can set the climate from my phone a few minutes before I get in so it's ready to go when I get there. I can monitor my cameras from home. All that, and it's super fast. The only things that might annoy you are: not many physical buttons (almost everything is done through a screen), the tires can wear out if you drive aggressively, and you'll get battery anxiety on long trips and don't properly plan for stops.

## 3. Qualify a disagreement

Meetings can be a waste of time if there's no clear agenda and there are people there who shouldn't be. On the other hand, meetings can really cut down on the back and forth you get with asynchronous communication; you can just ask direct questions, get direct answers, and hopefully not even use the entire time for the meeting.

## 4. Running late

Hey, I ran into some traffic so I'll be a few minutes late. My apologies, but there isn't much I can do. I'll be there ASAP.

## 5. Don't know / need a clarification

I'm not sure what you mean by PR; what do you mean by that?

## 6. Instructions

First, use your code on the door by pressing the buttons to light up the key pad, and then enter the code I gave you. The door should unlock. Once it's unlocked, come inside, please remove your shoes, and walk straight ahead. You'll pass the livingroom on your right and the kitchen will be straight ahead and to the right after that.

## 7. Decline / reschedule (actual Slack)

Yo, I might have a conflict at 11-11:30 on Monday for back to school stuff. So either I miss the Vercel follow up meeting or we reschedule to 1ish. Neither option bothers me much.

## 8. Recap of something that went sideways

Oh man, this weekend was crazy. The drain pipe to my upstairs shower came unhooked and gallons of water drained into the ceiling above my kitchen, which had no where to go but down into the walls on either side, which meant it came out the only path it could: the lighting fixtures, the electrical outlets, holes behind our cabinets, and the baseboards. Everything got wet. We dried as much as we could, but we couldn't reach everything. I have a fan blowing on it now, but we're going to need to get a water damage repair company, and we might have to reach out to insurance.

## 9. Explain a tool

Cursor is a text editor, primarily for programmers, that has a very tight integration with AI agents, not just in the editor where it will make really smart inline suggestions for you if you're trying to write out code. What's even more useful is that you can talk to it about your code base to ask it questions, draw diagrams, etc., or you can even plan out big code changes and have it implement them for you. You can decide how much or how little you want it to code for you. It can save a ton of time.

## 10. Mixed feelings

I really like ice cream, but it also spikes my blood sugar and causes an irregular heartbeat, so I don't have too much of it.

## 11. Ask for a favor

Hey, would you possibly write me a letter of recommendation? I hate to ask but a letter coming from you would go a long way. I would appreciate it more than you know.

## 12. Vent to a friend

Does this guy ever stop talking? Holy hell. I honestly don't think I've been able to get more than five words into this conversation. This guy must love to hear himself talk.

## 13. Praise (specific, after something hard)

Hey, the way Brad was speaking to you in that meeting was insane. I can't believe he felt comfortable laying into you like that. That being said, you handled it like a champ. Good on you for holding your ground.

## 14. Dry joke

Uh ... did you notice Mike always says "opposed to" instead of "as opposed to"? What a time saver.

## 15. Formal-ish email

## How I Work (excerpt)

Everyday work-doc voice. Full note lives in Scott's notes vault; this is the sample to match for docs like this.

I am in the Pacific time zone (UTC -8/-7). I start my mornings by getting my kids started with their day, then hopefully going for a run before starting work at around 9am. I try to take a lunch anywhere between 11:30am and 2pm. Sometimes I'll step away for a short sanity break. Other than that, I'm usually at my desk until around 6pm. At the very least, I have my phone with me, so I'm available for emergencies.

All that being said, I try my best to stay mindful, wherever I am. When I'm at work, my priority is work. When I'm with family, however, work becomes a close second.

Typically, I start my day out about 5-10 minutes early, just so I can prepare for the first meeting(s) of the day, get a sense of my priorities and determine what should get done.

As far as my approach for work, I treat every ticket as a little product. Before I start, I make sure the expected outcome and acceptance criteria are unambiguous. If they're not, I get that resolved rather than make assumptions.

In order for me to be successful in my work and my life, I aim for excellence in communication. Where it's lacking, I am open to feedback. That being said, I also prioritize communication along with other things that demand my attention on any given day: work, family, personal needs.

For meetings, I prefer quality over quantity. Too many meetings impede my ability to work efficiently. However, for unscheduled communication, I prefer Slack/Hangouts and then Email after that.

Though I try to stay vigilant in terms of getting back to people when they ask me for anything, should you feel I am taking too long, feel free to send me a message to remind me!

I sometimes get hyper-focused on a problem and have to remind myself to take a step back and look at the bigger picture.

Honest and constructive feedback is something I really value. If you feel like there's something I could improve on, I'd definitely like to know!

I aim for objective consistency with who I am in the world, which means I change my mind from time to time and outside perspectives are a huge help to that end.
