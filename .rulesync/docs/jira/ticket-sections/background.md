# Background

Provide context: what problem this solves, what variations or requirements exist, and links to designs or specs.

When the work replaces or improves an existing solution, include **pain points or "what doesn't work today"** about the current state. This grounds the reader in why the work is needed.

When citing a referenced doc (audit, spec, design RFC, prior ticket), prefer its **canonical Atlassian URL** (Confluence page, JIRA ticket, etc.) over a local repo path. JIRA renders smart-linkable URLs as inline preview cards, which reads better than a bare path that means nothing to most viewers. Local paths are the fallback when no smart-linkable source exists.

Break dense paragraphs at sentence boundaries when sentences carry distinct ideas -- a paragraph per idea is more skimmable than a wall of prose.
